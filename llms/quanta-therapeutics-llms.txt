# Quanta Therapeutics

> Privately held, clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company in South San Francisco, California
> (founded 2018, second site in Radnor, Pennsylvania) developing oral allosteric small-molecule
> medicines for RAS-driven cancers. Its proprietary high-throughput Second Harmonic Generation (SHG)
> optical platform detects protein conformational change to find allosteric modulators of signaling
> complexes, an approach the company calls conformational blockade of RAS. Clinical programs are
> direct multi-KRAS inhibitors: QTX3034 (G12D-preferring, Phase 1 monotherapy and with cetuximab),
> QTX3544 (G12V-preferring, IND cleared January 2025) and QTX3046 (first patient dosed June 2024),
> across pancreatic, colorectal, lung and endometrial cancers.
